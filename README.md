**Prompt for Website Generation:**

"Design a modern, professional, and visually engaging portfolio website for Anmol Soni. The design should be clean, responsive, and easy to navigate. Please include the following sections and content:

**1. Hero Section (Home):**

* **Name:** Anmol Soni
* **Short Introduction:** 'Hi, I'm Anmol. I am a first-year BBA student with a passion for blending data-driven insights with creative storytelling.'
* **Areas of Interest:** Business Analytics, Data Visualization, and Photography.
* **Call-to-Action (CTA):** Include two prominent buttons: 'View Projects' and 'Contact Me'.

**2. About Me (Education, Interests & Career Goals):**

* **Education:**
* BBA specializing in Data Analytics and Data Visualization (Samatrix) – Jaipur School of Business, JECRC University (Current).
* 12th Grade (Commerce Stream), CBSE Board – 86.7%.


* **Interests:** Candid and event photography, video editing, organizing events, playing Valorant, and motorcycle riding.
* **Career Goal:** To leverage my education in business administration and data analytics to innovate, grow, and eventually expand my family business.

**3. Skills Section:**

* Display a clean grid or list of my skills: Data Analytics, Data Visualization, Python (Beginner), Business Strategy, Event Organizing, Photography, and Video Editing.

**4. Projects Section:**

* Create a grid layout for project cards. Each card must include:
* **Project Name:** (e.g., 'JU Socialz Campus Documentation' or 'Python Data Analysis')
* **Description:** A brief 2-3 sentence overview of the project's impact and objective.
* **Technologies Used:** (e.g., Python, Excel, Adobe Lightroom).


**5. Contact & Footer Section:**

* A clean contact form or direct links.
* **Contact Info:** Include clear, clickable icons/text for my Email, LinkedIn, and GitHub profiles.

Please ensure the color palette is professional yet dynamic, and use modern typography."
